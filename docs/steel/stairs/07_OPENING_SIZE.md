# 07 Sizing the stair opening (DG34 §3.4.3 – 3.5)

Fixing the opening early is the most valuable single step. Late changes, or a non-standard stair built to fit, cost time and money. DG34 gives two sizing equations for a two-flight parallel stair (Fig. 3-3).

## Width [DG]

**W_open = 2 (edge gap) + 4 (stringer width) + 2 (egress width) + 1 (centre gap)**

| Term | Value |
|---|---|
| Edge gap (stringer to wall) | ≥ ¼ in. (6 mm); **½ in. (13 mm) recommended** against concrete or masonry. More where tolerances are large (13) |
| Stringer width | Channel: flange width, or **3 in. (75 mm)**. HSS: actual width, or **2 in. (50 mm)**. Plate: thickness, ≥ ½ in. at the wall; **2½ in. (65 mm)** at the inside stringer, which includes 2 in. for a side-mounted guard |
| Egress width | per code (06), less any allowed projections |
| Centre gap (between flights or guards) | ≥ ½ in. (13 mm), plus erection and fit-up room |

Examples with 44 in. egress, ½ in. gaps:
- channel stringers → **101½ in. (2.58 m)**;
- HSS → 97½ in. (2.48 m);
- plate, with ½ in. wall and 2½ in. inside stringers → 95½ in. (2.43 m). [REC] DG34 Ex. 3.1(c) prints 94½ in., but its own terms sum to 99½ in. Recompute; don't copy.

## Length [DG]

**L_open = L_stair + L_landing**

- **L_stair** = N_tread × tread + connection allowance. Allow **half a tread at each stringer end**, or the real connection length.
- **L_landing** = N_landing × (stringer width + egress width + end gap). End gap: ¼ in. minimum, ½ in. recommended.

Example: 10 treads × 11 in. + 2 × 5½ in. + one landing (3 + 44 + ½ in.) = **168½ in. (14 ft ½ in., 4.28 m)**. DG34 Ex. 10.1.1 uses a 6 in. connection allowance at one end and a 6 in. centre gap, giving 8 ft 11 in. × 13 ft 7½ in. (2.72 × 4.15 m).

## Why the half-tread allowance [DG]

- It gives room for **standard bolted shear connections** at the stringer ends (11), which are easier to erect than welded seats.
- The riser and nosing sit **back from the walking path** on the landing.
- A bigger offset gives a **smoother handrail transition** between flights. Agree it with the detailer.
- Welded connections need less room, but coordinate before counting on it.

## Add to the minimum [DG]

- Wall finishes, and **layers of fire-rated gypsum** in a stud enclosure.
- Concrete and masonry wall **position tolerance** (13).
- Fire-sprinkler standpipes and risers, the **area of rescue assistance**, and door positions and swings.
- The slab-edge support: angle or bent plate. The opening is usually set by the **slab-edge detail**, not by the beams (11).

## Adjustment instead of precision [DG]

- **Slab-edge angle with ± 1 in. (25 mm) adjustment** (Fig. 6-8). The final position is fixed during detailing with the architect and SER.
- Ship slab-edge angles **loose**, to be field-welded once the stair position is final.
- Use **extended single plates with horizontal slots** at stringer supports (Fig. 6-3).
- In existing buildings and concrete or masonry cores, **survey the as-built opening** before detailing.

## Office note [REC]

Dimension the opening on the floor plan: "MIN. CLEAR OPENING FOR STAIR S1, VERIFY BEFORE DETAILING". Keep the W_open and L_open terms in the design calc.
