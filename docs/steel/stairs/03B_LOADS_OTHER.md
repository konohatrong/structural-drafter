# 03B Environmental, thermal and integrity loads (DG34 §3.2.4, 3.2.6, 3.2.7)

Gravity loads → 03. Seismic → 04.

## Environmental loads on exterior stairs [CODE]

On an open exterior stair these can govern individual members.

| Load | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Wind** | ASCE 7 Ch. 26, usually §29.4 "other structures". Stair framing and guards are open, so the force coefficient Cf matters most: **use the lattice-framework values**. Minimum design pressure 16 psf (0.77 kPa) |
| **Snow** | ASCE 7 Ch. 7. At least 20 psf on solid treads and landings where snow applies; allow for drifting. In snowy regions the L + S combinations may govern. Detail drainage for melt |
| **Rain** | Sloped treads and landings usually need no check. Check long-span landings (where beam deflection can exceed the drainage slope), platforms with drains, and places where ice dams form (ASCE 7 Ch. 8) |
| **Ice** | Open stairs, catwalks and platforms are **ice-sensitive structures** (ASCE 7 Ch. 10). Add the ice weight, and the wind on the iced area |

## Thermal load [DG]

- Consider it for **long runs of exterior guards and handrails** with large temperature swings.
- Either design the members for the restrained thermal force, or **break the run with expansion joints or discrete lengths**.
- **Guard runs under 50 ft (15 m)** have historically given no trouble, and that is also a practical shipping length.
- Interior conditioned stairs see little temperature change in service. No check unless the project requires one; they do see some during construction.
- Stairs and gravity members are short and rarely matter. Where length change does matter, use **bearing or sliding connections**.
- References for joint spacing: Federal Construction Council, *Expansion Joints in Buildings* (1974); AISC Manual Part 2 "Thermal Effects". Basic thermal strain gives the length change and the stress.

## Structural integrity and notional loads [CODE]

- Every structure needs a **continuous load path** and a complete lateral force-resisting system (ASCE 7 §1.4).
- Stairs designed to ASCE 7 seismic provisions in **SDC B – F** normally satisfy §1.4 already.
- **In SDC A**, apply the notional lateral force **Fx = 0.01 Wx**, where Wx is the dead load assigned to the level, together with the §1.4 combinations. Connect each member to its supports.
- The stair's lateral path → 10.

## Office notes [REC]

- In Thailand, wind follows **DPT 1311**. Snow and ice do not apply.
- **Monsoon rain on exterior landings:** slope the platforms and treads, or use grating, and check long landings for ponding.
- **Galvanized exterior stairs:** see 13 for the detailing limits.
- **Long exterior guards** on stair towers: show the expansion-joint locations on the guard elevation, at no more than 15 m centres unless calculated.
