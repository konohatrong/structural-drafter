# 11B Connections to concrete and masonry (DG34 §6.3)

Stairs in concrete or masonry cores need more coordination than stairs in a steel frame. The SSE and the trades must agree on:
- the sequence;
- temporary support;
- wall and slab tolerances (13);
- who installs what;
- how field changes are handled.

**Detail for adjustment**, and make sure each trade knows how its work affects the stair connections.

## Embedded plates (cast-in concrete) [DG]

- Installed before the pour, and **detailed for adjustment** so the connecting pieces can shift in the field.
- **Left "clean"**, with nothing projecting, so no special formwork is needed. The connection material is **field-welded after stripping**. Fig. 6-14: built-up tee welded to the embed, erection bolts, all-round field weld.
- **Preferred in post-tensioned, pretensioned or heavily reinforced slabs**, where drilling for anchors is risky.
- Design the plate and its anchorage to **ACI 318**. In seismic design, anchorage uses Ωo (04).

## Beam pockets (masonry or concrete walls) [DG]

- Leave room to **place the member** and to reach the anchor rods or weld the base plate.
- **Sequence:**
  - set the steel first on **temporary supports** and build the wall around it; or
  - set it into pockets as the wall rises.
  Agree this with the trades.
- In masonry: a **bond beam** under the bearing, an embed plate, stiffeners and grout as required (Fig. 6-15). Design to **TMS 402 / ACI 530**.
- The SER must specify grouted cores, bond beams or extra reinforcement at pockets, embeds and anchors in the **building** documents.

## Post-installed anchors [DG]

- For walls and slabs **already built**: concrete or **fully grouted** masonry (Fig. 6-16, single angle with post-installed anchors).
- **Never into the face of hollow, ungrouted block:** the capacity is very limited.
- Mechanical or adhesive anchors, chosen for:
  - the design forces;
  - **seismic or wind qualification** (ACI 355.2 / evaluation reports);
  - compatibility with the base material;
  - approval by the authority.
- Design to ACI 318 and the manufacturer's data.

## Supporting elements may need changes [DG]

- A **thickened slab** under a stringer base on a slab on grade (Fig. 6-17: single angle with post-installed anchors).
- **Grouted cores** in masonry where anchors or posts land.
- Edge distances, and extra reinforcement round embeds and pockets.
- **Anchors under a stair pan:** leave access holes in the pan, or fit the pan after the anchors.

## Assembling in place [DG]

To absorb wall tolerances, stairs can ship as **individual pieces** and be assembled in the field. Fit is better, but erection takes longer.

## Office notes [REC]

- On the structural (building) drawings, show the **embed plates for the stair** with their mark, size, studs and position. Dimension them from the grid with a ± tolerance. Add "CO-ORDINATE WITH STAIR SHOP DRAWINGS".
- On the stair drawings, give each anchor type as "or approved equivalent with seismic qualification", with its embedment, edge distance and installation torque.
- Never show anchors into ungrouted block. Call out the grouted cells.
