# 12 Guards and handrails: members and parts (DG34 §7.1 – 7.2)

**Guard:** a barrier on the open sides of flights, landings and platforms, and under low soffits. **Handrail:** the grip along a flight, sometimes also on landings.

Geometry → 06; loads → 03; deflection → 05; connections and the post-to-stringer check → `12B_GUARD_CONNECTIONS.md`.

## Members [DG]

| Member | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Round HSS (preferred)** | ASTM **A500 Gr C (Fy 46 ksi)**, Gr B (42 ksi), or **A1085 (50 ksi, no wall-thickness reduction in design)**. Same sizes as pipe, higher strength. Often dual-certified; check availability |
| Pipe | ASTM A53 Gr B, **Fy 35 ksi**. Rails usually **1¼ in. (OD 1.660 in., 42 mm)** or **1½ in. (OD 1.900 in., 48 mm)**; Std, XS, XXS walls. Specify **"non-hydro"**, so the pipe has no unwanted test coatings |
| Square or rectangular HSS | Common with infill panels. Sizes below AISC Table 1-12: STI HSS Design Manual |
| Angle | Industrial platform guards, easy field welding. A large angle as top rail **fails graspability** |
| Plate, bar, rod | Architectural shapes, heavier |
| Wood, glass, aluminium, bronze, plastic | Often combined with steel, such as a wood top rail. Glass assemblies have extra code rules |

DG34 Ex. 10.1.7 (post spacing 4 ft, post 3.5 ft) uses:
- top rail and posts **HSS1.900×0.145**;
- handrail **HSS1.660×0.140**;
- pickets and brackets **½ × ½ in. bar**.

The **200 lb point load governs** the rails, not the 50 plf load.

## Guard parts [DG]

| Part | Function and design |
|---|---|
| **Top rail** | At the code height (06). Spans post to post for **vertical, lateral and axial** load. Strength checks: compression E3, shear G5 (rarely governs), flexure (Manual Table 3-14) |
| **Bottom rail** | Lower edge of the infill; set so the opening beneath stays within the code. Designed for the **infill** load |
| **Posts** | Carry the rails, infill and handrail. Cantilever: 200 lb at the rail height, e.g. Ma = 0.2 kip × 3.5 ft = 0.7 kip-ft. Deflection ≤ h/60. Mounted on the top or side of the stringer or landing framing |
| **Infill** | Pickets, panels, mesh, cables or extra rails. IBC: **no 4 in. sphere** through (larger in some occupancies); OSHA: **≤ 19 in.** least dimension. Avoid a **ladder effect** (climbable horizontals); the architect decides when this applies |
| **Handrail** | On the guard or on a wall, within the code height range. **Graspability** sets its size and shape. Spans support to support for vertical, lateral and axial load |
| **Toe plate** | Stops debris falling. **Required on OSHA stairs and platforms**, and wherever falling objects matter. Often omitted in offices; confirm with the architect. A support beam raised above the finished floor can act as the toe plate |

## Office notes [REC]

- Draw each guard type as an elevation: post spacing, rail and infill sizes, the opening limit as a dimension, handrail height above the nosing line, extensions.
- Give the guard **design loads** (200 lb / 50 plf / infill 50 lb on 300 × 300 mm) and the **deflection limits** in a stair criteria table (14).
- Specify rails as "round HSS A500 Gr C or A53 Gr B non-hydro pipe". Do not mix in a run without stating it.
