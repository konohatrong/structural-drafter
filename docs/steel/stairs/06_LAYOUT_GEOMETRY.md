# 06 Code geometry (DG34 §3.4, Table 3-8, Figs 3-1, 3-2)

The architect owns this geometry. The structure must fit **inside it without reducing any clear dimension**. The values are US (IBC 2015; the OSHA revision effective 17 January 2017). **On a Thai project, take them from Thai law and the architect**; they are listed here to show which dimensions the structure must protect.

## Points the structure must respect [DG + REC]

- **Clear width is measured between finished surfaces.** Stringers, guard posts on the inside face, handrail brackets and wall finishes all eat into it. Keep posts on top of the stringer, or allow for them (07).
- **Rise and run are finished dimensions.** Tile, terrazzo or nosings change the steel set-out. Get the finish thickness at every tread and landing before detailing.
- **Construction tolerance must not break uniformity** (⅜ in. in a flight). Adjust the support or the stair, never the riser count (13).
- **Headroom** under the flight above is often lost to beams, hangers and soffits. Check it in section.
- **Guard posts and the handrail extensions** need structure at the landing edges (12).
- **Avoid ladder-effect infill**, horizontal bars that children can climb, where the architect requires it (12).
