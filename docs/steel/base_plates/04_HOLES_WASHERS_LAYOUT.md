# 04 Holes, washers and rod layout (DG1 §2.6, §2.7)

The most common field problem is rods that do not fit the hole pattern or do not let the column be positioned. OSHA requires the EOR to approve any rod modification, so **make the holes as large as practical**.

- Washers (circular or square, cut from plate) **cover the hole with the rod at its edge**; thickness ≈ **⅓ rod diameter** for all F1554 grades. Leave **clearance** to the column and weld.

## Smaller hole option [DG]

For **compression-only** columns, a **1 1/16 in. (27 mm) hole for ¾ in. rods** in plates under 1¼ in. (32 mm) lets the holes be punched and **standard F844 washers** be used. Weigh the saving against rods set out of tolerance.

For moment or tension, **use Table 2.3**: a field fix of a fully stressed rod is very difficult.

## Washer rules [DG]

- **Do not weld washers to the plate**, except where the rods carry shear (15).
- **F436 washers are not used**: too small. Rod washers need not be hardened.

## Rod size and pattern [DG]

- Use **¾ in. F1554 Gr 36** wherever possible. For more strength, increase the diameter up to about **2 in.** in Gr 36 before changing grade.
- Specify **ample thread**: at least **3 in. (75 mm) more than required**, for setting variations.
- **Symmetric patterns, as few layouts as possible**; typically **four rods in a square**.
- **Edge distance:** where the hole edge carries no lateral force, as little as **½ in. (13 mm) clear** from hole to plate edge works, but more allows field slotting. Where it carries a lateral force, size it for that force.
- Allow **erector access** near walls. Show special settings on **both the column schedule and the foundation drawings**.

## Coordination with concrete [DG]

- Coordinate rods with the **reinforcement**, especially in **piers and walls**.
- **Rods in a pier must never extend below the pier into the footing**: they would have to be cast before the pier is formed, and alignment is lost.
- If the pier is shorter than the required embedment, **delete the pier** and extend the column down to the footing.
