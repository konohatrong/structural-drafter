# 18 Pretensioning and double-nut installation (DG1 App. A2, A2.1)

## When pretension is required [DG]

- Vibrating machine joints.
- **Double-nut joints** in **Seismic Design Category D or higher** (ASCE 7), or designed for fatigue.
- Poor tightening leads to uneven load sharing, loosening and fatigue failure; over-tightening strips threads.
- The contractor installs; the EOR may witness. Specify **vertical misalignment < 1:40** for fatigue rods. Use **templates of rings with nuts both sides at two levels**.

## Values [DG]

- **Minimum pretension Tm (Table A1):** 50 % of the minimum tensile strength for Gr 36; 60 % for Gr 55, Gr 105 and rebar. Examples: ¾ in. Gr 36 10 kips (44 kN), 1 in. Gr 36 18 kips (80 kN), 1 in. Gr 55 27 kips (120 kN).
- **Verification torque Tv = 0.12 db Tm** (in.-kips). Use 0.20 for less-lubricated rods.
- **Turn-of-nut (Table A2, UNC threads, from snug, + 20° tolerance):**

| Rod dia. | F1554 Gr 36 | Gr 55, 105, A615, A706 |
|---|---|---|
| ≤ 1½ in. | 1/6 turn | 1/3 turn |
| > 1½ in. | 1/12 turn | 1/6 turn |

## Double-nut installation, in order (A2.1) [DG]

1. Torque wrench calibrated yearly; certificate to the EOR.
2. **Rotation-capacity test** of one rod per lot, on a plate like the base plate. Reject the lot if the threads are damaged or Tv cannot be reached.
3. Fix the rods in a checked template; cast; remove a top template after 24 h.
4. Clean, lubricate if galvanized, inspect position, level, projection and threads; run the nuts by hand.
5. **Levelling nuts and washers**, levelled with the template. The gap from the nut bottom to the concrete is **≤ one rod diameter**.
6. **Beveled washers** where a face slopes more than **1:20** (Table A2 note: 1:40), or a nut cannot bear fully. They take up to 1:6.
7. Place the plate, washers and lubricated top nuts. Snug top nuts, then levelling nuts, to **20 – 30 % of Tv**, in a **star pattern**.
8. Mark the nuts, then turn the top nuts to the Table A2 rotation in **at least two star-pattern cycles**.
9. Check that **Tv** is needed to turn any nut further. If not, the thread has probably stripped: report it.
10. **After 48 h** (for cantilevers, 48 h after the rest of the structure is erected), check at **110 % of Tv**: galvanizing creeps.
11. For SDC D or fatigue: **lock the nuts** (jam nut), or check at 110 % Tv **every four years**.

**Tack welds:** only to the top of the top nut and rod, never from nut to washer or plate.

Sleeved joints, inspection and maintenance: 18B.
