# 18B Sleeved joints, inspection and maintenance (DG1 App. A2.2, A3)

## Sleeved pretensioned joints (A2.2) [DG]

The first steps of 18, plus: clean and seal the sleeve; tension with a **centre-hole ram**, run the nut down, release; expect some relaxation. The EOR specifies the procedure.

## Inspection after installation (A3) [DG]

Regularly for fatigue joints, and after a significant earthquake for SDC D joints:
1. **Appearance:** number the rods clockwise; look for corrosion (worse below the concrete if wet), gouges, cracks (dye penetrant). Check washers, levelling nuts, bent rods, and thread projection. **No lock washers.** No galvanized nuts on unpainted weathering steel.
2. **Sound** each rod with a ball-peen hammer: a dull ring means broken or loose.
3. **Tightness:** check the locking device or apply 110 % Tv.
   - One loose nut: clean, inspect, retighten by turn-of-nut.
   - More than one: **disassemble, replace the nuts**, and watch the joint.
4. **Ultrasonic test** only after welded repairs, if similar structures have cracked, or if fatigue was not designed for.

Also keep the joint free of debris, water and plants.
