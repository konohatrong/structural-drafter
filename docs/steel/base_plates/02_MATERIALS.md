# 02 Materials (DG1 §2.1, §2.2, §2.5)

## Base plates (Table 2.1) [DG]

| Thickness tp | Available grades |
|---|---|
| ≤ 4 in. (100 mm) | **A36 (preferred)**, A572 Gr 42 or 50, A588 Gr 42 or 50 |
| 4 – 6 in. (100 – 150 mm) | **A36 (preferred)**, A572 Gr 42, A588 Gr 42 |
| > 6 in. (150 mm) | A36 |

- Use **A36** unless another grade is confirmed available. High-strength plate is seldom worth it: a thicker plate gives the strength.
- **Material is cheaper than labour.** A thicker plate usually beats stiffeners. Exception: large moment bases (crane buildings), where a **stool** may avoid CJP welds to heavy plates (17).
- Plates come in ⅛ in. steps to 1¼ in., ¼ in. steps above. **Standardise** plate sizes in the design.
- [REC] Thai equivalent: **SS400** (JIS G3101 / TIS) plate, Fy 245 MPa for t ≤ 16 mm, lower for thicker plate; state the grade on the drawing.

| ASTM | Fu ksi (MPa) | Max dia. in. | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| **F1554 Gr 36** | 58 (400) | 4 | **Preferred**, the default; weldable |
| F1554 Gr 55 | 75 (517) | 4 | Large tension (moment bases, overturning) |
| F1554 Gr 105 | 125 (862) | 3 | Only when larger Gr 36 / 55 rods cannot work |
| A449 | 120 / 105 / 90 | 1 / 1½ / 3 | Older spec, for comparison |
| A36, A307 | 58 | 4 | Older specs, few rod-specific requirements |

Nominal stresses on the unthreaded body: Fnt = 0.75Fu; Fnv = 0.50Fu (threads excluded), 0.40Fu (included). Current AISC values: 01.

## F1554 details [DG]

- **Colour code:** Gr 36 blue, Gr 55 yellow, Gr 105 red. Gr 36 is often uncoded.
- Threads UNC Class 2A unless specified.
- **Nuts: heavy hex**, A563 Gr A (DH for Gr 105), especially with oversized holes.
- Supplied straight (threaded, nut anchorage), bent or headed. Rods to about 1 in. may have forged heads.
- **Use headed rods or rods with a nut.** Hooks have very limited pullout strength (11).
- **No plate washers at the embedded end** as a rule: they add no pullout strength and obstruct bars and concrete. Exception: high-strength rods or side blowout, by calculation.
- **Gr 55 with supplement S1** (carbon equivalent ≤ 0.45 %) if field welding may be needed.
- **CVN supplements S4 / S5** are seldom worth it: 95 % of fatigue life is used before a crack is a few millimetres long. Add redundancy instead (17B).

## Galvanizing [DG]

- Hot-dip (A153) or mechanical (B695), but **rod and nut by the same process**, bought from one supplier and **shipped preassembled**. Put this in the contract documents; ASTM does not require it.
- Galvanizing raises nut friction; lubrication may be needed.

## Other anchors [DG]

- **Drilled-in epoxy (adhesive) rods** are acceptable, for fast-track jobs and repairs (05, 07).
- **Wedge-type expansion anchors are not recommended**: they must be tensioned to lock and loosen when the column moves during erection.

## Office note [REC]

Name one rod grade per project where possible, and give the grade, diameter, length, thread length, nut and washer on the anchor rod schedule (20).
