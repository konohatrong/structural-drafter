# 05 Rod placement and tolerances (DG1 §2.8)

Proper rod placement gives safe, fast and economical erection.

## Layout drawing [DG]

- Start with an **anchor rod layout drawing**. Problems are fewer when the **steel detailer** coordinates every rod with its base plate, rather than setting out from the foundation drawings and column schedule alone.
- It shows **all rod marks, layout dimensions and elevations**.
- **Use only drawings "Released for Construction"** for setting rods, never a drawing submitted for approval.
- Layout and as-built survey by an **experienced construction surveyor** who reads structural drawings. A land surveyor may lack the experience.

## Templates [DG]

- One template per rod pattern. **Plywood** templates on site are cheap and nail to the forms. A **nut on each side** of the template holds each rod straight.
- **Steel templates** (plates or angle frames) for large assemblies with tight tolerances; give them nail holes. They can be reused as setting plates (06).
- **Embedded templates** keep large assemblies aligned during the pour. Keep them small, place the rods first and the bars around them, and **consolidate the concrete** under them, especially if the template is part of the anchorage.
- After the templates are removed: survey the rods, **mark grid lines on each setting**, clean them, check that the nuts turn and the rods are plumb, lubricate the threads if needed.
- **OSHA:** the contractor reviews the settings and **notifies the EOR of any rod that will not meet the tolerance** for the specified hole.

## Tolerances: AISC Code of Standard Practice §7.5.1 [CODE]

| Item | Tolerance |
|---|---|
| (a) Between centres of two rods in a group | ⅛ in. (3 mm) |
| (b) Between centres of adjacent groups | ¼ in. (6 mm) |
| (c) Elevation of the rod tops | ± ½ in. (13 mm) |
| (d) Accumulated, along a column line | ¼ in. per 100 ft (6 mm per 30 m), max. 1 in. (25 mm) |
| (e) Group centre to the established column line | ¼ in. (6 mm) |

- **ACI 117** allows **± 1 in. (25 mm)** for embedded items: far looser.
- Each trade works to its own standard unless told otherwise. **Require the AISC tolerances for anchor rods in the concrete specification** (CSI Division 3), and **list the values** rather than only citing the document.

## Fast-track and complex layouts [DG]

- Where the steel design lags the foundations, or the layout is too complex to set accurately in forms, use **drilled-in epoxy (adhesive) rods** instead of cast-in rods.
- Foundations start without waiting for rods, and layout is easier and more accurate.

## Office note [REC]

- Put the five tolerances in the anchor rod general notes, in mm, with "SET ANCHOR RODS TO AISC 303 §7.5.1; ACI 117 TOLERANCES DO NOT APPLY TO ANCHOR RODS".
- Issue the anchor rod layout as its own sheet with rod marks, coordinates to grids and top-of-rod levels.
- Steel S8.4 requires setting by template; this file gives the reasons.
